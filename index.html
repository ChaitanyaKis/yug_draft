    Before silicon
    
          
    Every track has an ancestor.
    
        
    
        
    Each of Yugantra's four competition tracks starts from an idea this subcontinent had long before the machine. One of them was worked out here in Kerala. All four run live below.
    
      
    

      
      
        
    1. 
          
      c. 200 BCE
      
          
      Pingala
      
          
      The Chandas Shastra lists every metre as a string of light and heavy syllables, row by row. That's binary counting in all but name.
      
          
      
          
        
      2. 
        
    2. 
          
      1st–3rd century CE
      
          
      The Arthashastra
      
          
      A treatise on statecraft, economic policy and trade, attributed to Kautilya. Business thinking, written down.
      
          
      
          
        
      3. 
        
    3. 
          
      c. 600 CE
      
          
      Chaturanga
      
          
      A war game from Gupta-era India: an army of four divisions on an uncheckered 8 × 8 board, and the ancestor of chess.
      
          
      
          
        
      4. 
        
    4. 
          
      683 CE · Kerala
      
          
      Katapayadi
      
          
      Kerala astronomers wrote numbers as words: each consonant stands for a digit, and the digits are read backwards. A code you can remember, centuries before passwords.
